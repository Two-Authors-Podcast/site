DOUG: Nick does it the other way.

NICK: I've done that.

MELANIE: I didn't feel like setting all that up, so I just use my website, but you can also use BookFunnel. When they hit confirm, they're taken straight to the scene, and there's nothing else they have to do. DOUG: That's kind of cool. Nick, you should do one for AJ with a map of the different dive sites. That'd be neat.

MELANIE: Call it a bonus scene, a peek at their future, something like that. The book needs to feel like a complete experience. DOUG: I've been treating it like the end of Marvel movies, where you get to the end of the credits and there's that extra scene that mostly doesn't matter to the story. It's just a bonus. ## Which bonus scenes bring in new readers

## Bonus scene or pre-order first?

NICK: I remember what I was going to say. I don't do a bonus in every release any more, because my series tend to be longer. When I do, it's the first call to action: they sign up, go to the website, and at the bottom of the bonus there's the link to pre-order the next book. When I compare that with having the pre-order as the first call to action and no bonus, I get more pre-orders when the pre-order is the immediate call to action, even though it's right at the bottom of the bonus. It's not a huge difference, but it's five to ten per cent fewer pre-orders.

MELANIE: I'd believe it. The first call to action when somebody finishes a book is what you're going to get. So you have a choice to make: would you rather have the pre-order for the next book, or the person on your list? It's a hard decision. For me, at least at this point, I want that person on my list, because I'll have multiple opportunities to get them to pre-order. If they're on my list, I have them for a long time, and I have a way to communicate with them outside Amazon or any other platform I don't own. So an email is pretty valuable to me, maybe as valuable as a pre-order. That's why I've prioritised it.

DOUG: You could make your first automation after greeting them a reminder not to forget to pre-order the next one. That might at least increase your percentage.

MELANIE: I do have the pre-order there. I have the bonus scene opt-in to get on my newsletter list, and then I have what's coming next.
